How we win adoption

The NHS adopts by evolution, not revolution.

Disruptive products stall on “who are you to tell us?”. We reframe them so buyers feel they self-selected best practice - a positioning playbook proven across GP triage, hospital service lines, and CQC tooling. That reframe is usually worth more than any change to the product itself.
