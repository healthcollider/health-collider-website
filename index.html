Questions founders ask us first.

How do I get my health-tech product adopted in healthcare?

It turns on three things: a payer with budget, someone who can sign, and a proposition that reads as best practice rather than disruption. Most founders solve one and assume the rest follow.

We already have an advisor. Why would we need you?

Most advisors sell one slice - compliance here, sales there, nobody joining them up. We cover clinical, commercial and regulatory as one team, and we are on the buyer's side of the table in our day jobs.

What does a healthcare market access consultancy actually do?

Maps how money reaches your product, identifies who decides, and positions it so healthcare buyers can say yes. For us that also means routes into ICBs and PCNs, and a clinician community that can supply pilot sites and case studies.

How quickly does anything actually happen?

A session summary lands within five working days, a full assessment in four to six weeks. Scope and dates are agreed in writing first.
