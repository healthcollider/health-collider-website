The view from the buyer's chair

Most health-tech founders think the NHS is one customer. It is dozens of mini-markets, each with its own rulebook, its own budget, and its own reasons to say "not this year". The blocker is rarely the technology. It is access, positioning, and whether value ever translates into sustainable funding.

That is the work we do. We have built and scaled health businesses ourselves and we sit on the committees that decide what gets adopted, so the advice is lived, not theoretical.

The NHS adopts by evolution, not revolution. We reframe a product so buyers feel they self-selected best practice.

That reframe removes the "who are you to tell us?" barrier - and it is usually worth more than any change to the product itself. It is a positioning playbook proven across GP triage, hospital service lines, and CQC tooling.
