About

Accelerating healthcare innovation into practice.

Health Collider is a health-tech consultancy founded and run by practising NHS clinicians. We help health-tech companies win market access and market share in the UK NHS.

Why clients choose us

The advice is lived, not theoretical.

We're practising NHS clinicians who've built, exited, and evaluated health-tech companies - thirty years between us building and selling health technology, and practising in the NHS that buys it. The outcome we deliver is market access - increased NHS market share - and we win it the way the NHS actually adopts: by evolution, not revolution. We reframe disruptive products so buyers feel they self-selected best practice, which removes the “who are you to tell us?” barrier.
