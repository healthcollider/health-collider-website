  • The red flags an NHS buyer would spot first

What we need from you

A short form, not a data room. Tell us about the offer - a deck is optional.

  • A short description of what your product does

  • How you make money - SaaS subscription, per-user or per-site licensing, transaction-based, equity/profit share, or your own model

Before you submit.

What if I don't have a pitch deck?

You don't need one. Tell us what you do and who you're selling to in the form - a deck just gives us more to go on if you've got a recent one.

How is this different from the Market Access Project?

The Market Access Project is a full seven-domain readiness assessment over several weeks, from about £10,000. NHS Fit Check is a five-day, fixed-price read on your offer - buyer, incentives, clinical fit, and where it might go to market.

What happens after?

You get the PDF report. If it points to gaps worth fixing properly, we'll say so - and £395 comes off a Market Access Project if you commission one within 90 days.
