Before you join.

How much time does it take?

Most opportunities are a single hour or less - a questionnaire, or one interview. Ongoing advisory roles exist but are always agreed with you up front. You reply only to what interests you, and there is no minimum.

Who pays me, and when?

The company pays you directly, at the rate they set and state before you accept. Health Collider places the opportunity and takes nothing from your fee.

Do I need to declare this work?

Paid advisory work is a declarable interest under GMC good practice guidance and under most NHS employers' policies. Declare it through your usual route, keep your own record of what you advised on, and never share patient-identifiable information.

Who can join?

Any GMC-registered doctor in current UK practice, at any grade and in any specialty. We verify your GMC number against the public register before your first opportunity.
